## Regression checklist (run after each meaningful change)

- Core acceptance tests 5, 7, 15, 17 still pass.
- `EMAIL_LOG.IdempotencyKey` unique per SENT row.
- LH cannot access another LH's client via direct RPC (test 25).
- `AUDIT_LOG` has fresh rows for every mutation you just did.
- `Admin → Automation` shows *tick trigger installed = YES*.

## Known limitations

- Apps Script triggers can drift by up to 5 minutes; jobs may fire slightly after their nominal time.
- Gmail quota is per account (~1500 recipients/day for Workspace); heavy months should be spread across days or use a delegated account with higher quota.
- The web app is intended for a Crux-domain audience; exposing it as *Anyone with link* is possible but disables identity-based scoping.
